The analytical approach

Question: Do training and on-road training receipts cover the recruiting and onboarding effort for a defined cohort?

Design: Bring recruiting services and staff allocations together with trainee, training driver and rider labor, uniforms and applicable revenue-based taxes. Keep receipts and costs visible before calculating the net result. A negative result identifies a modeled onboarding investment; it does not by itself establish that hiring is uneconomic over the employee's tenure.

Scope: This is its own profitability analysis. Costs may already exist in payroll or expense reporting; displaying them here does not create an additional charge in dispatch costing. The demonstration makes no claim of implementation or measured savings.

How to establish accuracy

  • Match receipts, trainees, sessions and labor to the same cohort and reporting period.
  • Reconcile recruitment staff allocations to documented time and payroll.
  • Verify trainee, driver and rider hours and rate composition, including overtime, PTO, taxes and benefits.
  • Trace uniforms and recruiting charges to invoices; confirm sign conventions and eligible categories.
  • Confirm training receipts and applicable revenue-based taxes using approved records.
  • Bridge this analysis to payroll and expenses without posting its totals as an additional dispatch expense.

These are arithmetic checks on fictional inputs. Source reconciliation and policy approval remain pending.
