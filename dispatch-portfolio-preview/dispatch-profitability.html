Accrual versus actual spending

The $1.50 rate is the owner-confirmed model rule, not a forecast of actual repair expense. Eligible spending consumes the reserve; it is not charged again alongside the accrual in the same cost view. A negative balance signals a reserve shortfall.

What the reserve means for the example route

A's 8 hours and C's 1 sweep hour would create 9 van-hours only if they each use a van on the road for those hours. Under that explicit fictional assumption, the separate damage accrual is 9 × $1.50 = $13.50. The agreed example's $184 profit is before this additional reserve; after the reserve it is $170.50. Before live use: reconciliation checklist

Reconcile each driver's paid-duty hours and eligible costs to the same payroll week. Resolve overtime, breaks, training, PTO, taxes and benefits. Trace every pool to ledger or invoice evidence, verify period conversions, and assign each expense exactly once.

When a starting bills-and-supplies total already contains fleet loss, move that component into the fleet pool and remove it from operating costs so combined cost stays unchanged. Reconcile fleet reimbursements once, and confirm coverage for maintenance, parts and repairs. Reconcile hiring vendor costs and recruitment/training staff allocations to existing pools to avoid duplicate recovery.

Match route revenue and all primary/sweep time to source records. Require a unique approved sweep ID with driver, route, date and interval; reject overlaps and duplicates. Reconcile all route and non-route hours to each driver's weekly hours. Review rounding, exclusions and exceptions; obtain accounting and operational sign-off.

05 / From company averages to dispatch decisions

Problem and investigation

Dispatch needed individual driver costs to evaluate route profitability. The prior workbook review traced payroll, hours, PTO, L&I and shared expenses. The supplied review summary identified a common wage in place of individual costs, inconsistent hour denominators, and external expenses whose weekly conversion could not be verified.

These findings are carried forward from the supplied prior review, not a new workbook audit. The original workbook was not opened or changed for this build.

Proposed design and validation

Use each driver's eligible weekly costs and paid-duty hours, then add separately visible shared pools over a consistent fleet-wide denominator. Include verified sweep assistance as its own driver-time cost. Confirmed demonstration rules are distinct from unresolved company policies.

The agreed example produces A $52/hour, B $54/hour and C $50/hour. A's eight hours plus C's one-hour sweep yield $184 modeled profit on $650 revenue. Full-week allocations reconcile to $6,260; this single route consumes only nine of the week's 120 hours.

Honest outcome

Identified inconsistencies described in the prior review and designed a separate, testable framework for individual driver costing and route profitability. This is a fictional portfolio demonstration of a proposal. It does not establish actual implementation or measured savings.
